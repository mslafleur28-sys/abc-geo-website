Founder & Author

Kayla LaFleur – Founder & Author

Geospatial & GEO Specialist · Creator of the A+B=GEO formula

At a glance

Kayla LaFleur is the founder of abcGEO and the creator of the proprietary A+B=GEO formula—Entity + Transitive Verb = machine-readable GEO output— used to structure geospatial content and tools for Generative Engine Optimization.

Background

Kayla works at the intersection of geospatial data engineering, web mapping, and search-era visibility. Her practice centers on converting spatial formats (GeoJSON, KML, Shapefile, CSV) into reliable, browser-ready layers; building interactive maps with modern web stacks; and writing answer-first technical content that AI crawlers can extract and cite with confidence.

Through abcGEO she publishes pillar guides, conversion utilities, and GEO methodology so developers and GIS teams can ship spatial products that perform in both traditional search and generative answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

Proprietary frameworks

  • Creator of the A+B=GEO formula — abcGEO’s core methodology pairing a named entity with a transitive verb so LLM crawlers extract unambiguous, citable facts.
  • Extractable RAG Architecture — editorial and schema patterns that make geospatial and GEO content chunkable for retrieval-augmented answer engines.
  • INSTASTACK & Citationscape tooling — interactive utilities that demonstrate GEO claims in the browser while reinforcing the same entity graph in schema and copy.

Areas of expertise

  • Geospatial data conversion and spatial data engineering
  • Interactive web mapping (Leaflet, MapLibre, Mapbox-style workflows)
  • Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) for AI search citation
  • Schema.org markup for TechArticle, Person, and SoftwareApplication entities
