How to build topical authority

  • Pillar Hub (P1): one broad, authoritative overview of the primary subject.
  • Sub-Clusters (C1.1, C1.2…): 3–5 core sub-topics that expand specific pillar sections.
  • Micro-Clusters (M1.1a, M1.1b…): 2–3 long-tail articles—how-tos, comparisons, or direct Q&A—under each cluster.
  • Answer-first (AEO): the opening section of every URL answers the Direct Core Question in the row.
  • Linking: micros point up to their sub-cluster; sub-clusters point up to the pillar; the pillar links down to every cluster.

Recommended workflow

  1. Generate a starter outline from your niche, or start from a blank worksheet.
  2. Rewrite titles and keywords until each row owns one distinct intent.
  3. Confirm parent link targets form closed loops—no orphans.
  4. Export Markdown and assign priorities (P1 first, P2 next).
