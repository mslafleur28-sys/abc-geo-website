How Answer Engines Resolve Entities via Citation Graphs

    

      Answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity do not merely rank pages—they
      resolve named entities against a knowledge graph before citing a brand. When your site
      publishes an unambiguous JSON-LD @graph that links an
      Organization to its founders (Person), offerings
      (Service), industry topics (knowsAbout), and external
      verification URLs (sameAs), retrieval systems can ground citations in
      structured facts instead of inventing relationships.
    

    

      A citation graph is the visual counterpart of that schema. The brand sits at the center;
      edges encode schema.org predicates that AI crawlers already understand. Citationscape
      Structures both layers—visual and machine-readable—so your brand entity is
      disambiguated across the open web.

        profiles so knowledge graphs merge your brand with existing entity IDs.
