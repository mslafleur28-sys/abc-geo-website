Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring web content so AI engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini) can extract and cite facts easily. By applying the formula A (Entity) + B (Transitive Verb) = GEO Result, developers and writers create unambiguous, machine-readable relationship statements that AI bots prioritize over standard keyword density.

1. The Death of Keyword Density

Traditional SEO rewarded websites that repeated target keywords and built backlink networks. Generative AI engines operate on semantic understanding—they read entire documents and measure the factual density of the text.

Key Takeaways

  • ✓ AI models prioritize high fact-to-word ratios over raw article length.
  • ✓ Answer-first formatting yields significantly higher citation rates in Perplexity & ChatGPT.
