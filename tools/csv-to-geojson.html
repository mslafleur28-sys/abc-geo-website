Answer-first

The abcGEO CSV to GeoJSON Converter maps longitude/latitude (or lon/lat) columns into Point features, or reads simple POINT WKT, and emits a FeatureCollection with remaining columns as properties.

Convert CSV lat/lon points to GeoJSON

  • Auto-detects common header names: longitude/latitude, lon/lat, lng, x/y.
  • Keeps non-coordinate columns as GeoJSON properties.
  • Supports quoted CSV fields and UTF-8 text.
  • Exports ready-to-map JSON for Leaflet and MapLibre.

How to use this converter

  1. Ensure your CSV has a header row and longitude/latitude columns.
  2. Paste or upload the file, then click Convert.
  3. Verify a sample feature’s coordinates are [lon, lat], not swapped.
  4. Download GeoJSON or continue to GeoJSON to KML / CSV tools as needed.
