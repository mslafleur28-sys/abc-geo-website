GeoJSON to KML Converter

Converts GeoJSON FeatureCollections into Keyhole Markup Language (KML) for Google Earth, Maps, and visualization workflows—entirely in your browser.

GeoJSON input

KML output

Answer-first

The abcGEO GeoJSON to KML Converter converts RFC 7946 GeoJSON (points, lines, polygons, and multi-geometries) into OGC KML Placemarks you can open in Google Earth—coordinates stay longitude, latitude.

Convert GeoJSON to KML for Google Earth and web maps

  • Preserves feature names from common property keys (name, title, id).
  • Supports Point, LineString, Polygon, and Multi* geometries.
  • Runs locally in the browser—your file is not uploaded to a server.
  • Pairs with the KML to GeoJSON tool for round-trip checks.

How to use this converter

  1. Paste GeoJSON or upload a .geojson / .json file (or load the sample).
  2. Click Convert to generate KML Placemarks inside a Document.
  3. Copy or download the .kml and open it in Google Earth to verify.
  4. Need the reverse path? Use the KML to GeoJSON converter next.
