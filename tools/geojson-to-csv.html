GeoJSON to CSV Converter

Converts GeoJSON FeatureCollections into spreadsheet-friendly CSV—longitude/latitude for points, or WKT geometry for lines and polygons.

GeoJSON input

CSV output

Answer-first

The abcGEO GeoJSON to CSV Converter flattens feature properties into columns and adds longitude/latitude for Point layers, or a WKT column when geometries are mixed or non-point.

Export GeoJSON features to CSV

  • Point-only collections get longitude and latitude columns.
  • Mixed geometries export WKT plus geometry_type.
  • Property keys become CSV headers automatically.
  • Ideal for analysts who live in spreadsheets and BI tools.

How to use this converter

  1. Paste or upload GeoJSON (FeatureCollection preferred).
  2. Convert to generate CSV with UTF-8 text.
  3. Open in Excel, Sheets, or a database import tool.
  4. Round-trip points with CSV to GeoJSON when needed.
