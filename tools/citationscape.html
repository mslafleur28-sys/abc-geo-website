How Answer Engines Resolve Entities via Citation Graphs

          

            Answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity do not merely rank pages—they
            resolve named entities against a knowledge graph before citing a brand. When your site
            publishes an unambiguous JSON-LD @graph that links an
            Organization to its founders (Person), offerings
            (Service), industry topics (knowsAbout), and external
            verification URLs (sameAs), retrieval systems can ground citations in
            structured facts instead of inventing relationships.
          

          

            A citation graph is the visual counterpart of that schema. The brand sits at the center;
            edges encode schema.org predicates that AI crawlers already understand. Citationscape
            Structures both layers—visual and machine-readable—so your brand entity is
            disambiguated across the open web.
          

          
    
            
  • 
              Organization @id — A stable IRI (usually your canonical URL plus
              #organization) that other nodes reference, preventing duplicate brand nodes.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              founder / Person — Ties an executive name and job title to the brand so
              engines can answer “who founded…” without scraping bio pages alone.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              knowsAbout — Declares industry or niche topics the brand is authoritative on,
              improving topical entity matching in RAG pipelines.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              makesOffer / Service — Lists core products so answer engines can cite
              specific offerings rather than a vague “company that helps with…”.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              sameAs — Crosswalks Wikipedia, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and YouTube
              profiles so knowledge graphs merge your brand with existing entity IDs.
            
    • 
          

          

            Embed the generated <script type="application/ld+json"> block in your
            homepage <head>, keep sameAs URLs live and consistent, and
            restate the same entity–predicate–object triples in visible HTML. That alignment is what
            Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) requires for reliable brand citation.
